\section{$\!\!\!\!\!\!\!$. Conclusion and Discussion}    % Section 6

In this paper, we have proposed the Bernstein--binomial (\BernBino) distribution, which replaces the fixed beta prior of the beta--binomial model with a Bernstein polynomial prior that can approximate any continuous prior density on $[0,1]$, and we have established its theoretical properties and developed both frequentist and Bayesian inferential procedures. The simulation studies in \S4 demonstrated the accuracy of the MM algorithm for maximum likelihood estimation, the reliability of the sandwich and bootstrap confidence intervals, the superiority of the \BernBino\ distribution in fitting complex mixed prior structures, and the efficiency and convergence of the DA algorithm for Bayesian inference. The real data analysis in \S5 on the low--iron rat teratology data further confirmed that the \BernBino\ distribution adequately captures the extra--binomial variation of real over--dispersed binomial data, remains competitive with mainstream mixture models, and provides a smooth, bounded and interpretable estimate of the prior density of the success probability. Extending the proposed model to a regression framework with covariates, as outlined in \S2.5, and to other discrete distributions defined on finite sets are interesting topics for future research.
